A FIELD GUIDE TO WANDERING

The universe,
at your own pace.

There’s no right direction. Start with a world that catches your eye.

01

Find your perspective

Drag to orbit the scene. Scroll or pinch to move closer. On a keyboard, use + and − to zoom.

02

Get to know a world

Select a planet or its name, then choose Discover for a close-up. Use the arrow keys to visit the next planet.

03

Let time unfold

Watch the planets move at their relative orbital speeds. At 1×, one simulated day lasts 100 seconds. Fast-forward accelerates both rotation and orbits. Press Space to pause the moment. Enable Drift for a slowly changing perspective.

Choose full motion for flowing camera travel and optional drift. Reduced motion uses instant transitions.

H opens immersive view. M toggles ambient sound. T starts or stops the journey. Escape returns you to the controls. Sizes, distances, and starting positions are arranged for exploration; this is not a live sky chart.

A beautiful approximation

Planet textures show real surface features. Orbital periods, eccentricities, and axial tilts inform the simulation. Planet sizes and orbital distances are adapted to keep every world within reach. Starting positions are illustrative.

Temperatures are approximate global means or, for gas and ice giants, atmospheric values at 1 bar. The Moon shows its surface temperature range; the Sun shows its photosphere temperature.
